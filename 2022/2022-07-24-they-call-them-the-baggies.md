# they call them the baggies

fiction

edward w pritchard

When a stock has crashed and former investors keep hanging on , desperately buying more and more to average down on manipulated dips and buying occasionally more, more, more  on the ravings of mad men;  they call those hangers on,  those gullible saps,  the baggies, that is bag holders, holding a bag of worthless garbage. Baggies are all delusional but in a nice, hopeful, patient way.

America has crashed and former patriots keep hanging on desperately becoming more and more optomistic

desperately hoping that  a decaying document of 18th century values will rejuvenate  a dying nation.

Dismantle the war machines and worship of wealth America- times have changed. Listen to the falling soft rains, a few minutes pre dawn. Rain, the greatest commodity in the universe, to erase our sins.
