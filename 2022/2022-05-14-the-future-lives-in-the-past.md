# the future lives in the past

fiction

edward w pritchard

The future lives in the past.

Rather than electric, hydrogen or gasoline being the future of the auto industry and peoples preferences of perpetual locomotion economic and environmental  circumstances will dictate  trains as the new source of locomotion for Americans.

Heed the future. Look for trains as the next best thing.
