# choose your psychological make up

fiction

edward w pritchard

Should you base your behavior and psychological makeup on Neitzche's writings and similar philosophies   or the Good Summarian? That seems to be the confrontation facing ordinary Americans and wrong choices among many can lead to

an unhealthy breakdown in the social order. Which seems to be happening now.

Help others, harm no one, and force yourself if necessary to be cordial with strangers. If your moods lead you to be two faced like Janus keep the cynic in yourself and display in public your positive proclivities the best you can.

I am starting today. Join me.
