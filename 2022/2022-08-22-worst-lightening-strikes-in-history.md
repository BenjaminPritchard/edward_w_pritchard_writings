# worst lightening strikes in history

fiction

edward w pritchard

Lightening frightens us all. It's not unusual to have reports unverified and less than scientific that lightning killed up to 300 persons in ancient babylonian times or native americans at youngstown ohio sometime in the past. Of course there are no scientific provable witness collaborations. Although there at Youngstown there is a large rock that was split !/3 2/3rds by gods' wrath, lightning.

But what of lightning strikes on distant galaxies that perhaps wiped up to billions [ 300 billion] life sources?

sometime in the distant past with no witnesses or collaboration. A tragedy indeed long since forgotten or unknown.

Don't carry an umbrella in a lightning storm. There is a connection between god's moods and lightning deaths among sentient beings. Be safe around lightning, although the odds of being emulsified are low its an absurd way to go.
