part 1

Shelley Fabares that’s a strange hair do, pink, yellow, red, all at once. I liked it better the old way. Like before. When you were singing on TV and the radio.

I know you don’t know me but I remember you. Why did you have to change your hair Shelley?

part 2

Shelley fabres/part 2

Why does one so beautiful have to age? I know you are beautiful on the inside too. But we never really got to know you. Admiration from afar only. Still I must add something, don’t take it wrong, it was a long time ago.

First you always looked so ladylike. Even in a bikini. I certainly liked it. But that pose of you as a blond, front on, well it might have been a little too much.

Still, did you ever do any can-can or Andrews sister type world war two stuff in a very tight long skirt. Just asking. Sometimes you make my mind wander Shelley.
