Note: author has been using Jack the Ripper’s visits metaphorically as part of his subconscious that visit only now and then. see parts 1-16 previous

Jack the ripper: So you see what I mean

Author: yes I am afraid so, about Mary Jane Kelly

Jack the ripper- you should write about it.

Author- tonight, I know that your last victim Mary Jane Kelly was an attractive girl, but was she intelligent?

Jack When you are ready to write tonight, ask me then. Meanwhile see to your workouts, it’s good for you, [chuckling] and whispering in a sad sardonic but quiet way, “sublimate my amigo”
