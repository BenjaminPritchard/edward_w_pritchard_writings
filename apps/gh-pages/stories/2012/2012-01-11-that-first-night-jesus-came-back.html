that first night Jesus came back

📅 2012-01-11 📖 102 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
#religion
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

That first night Jesus came back he really wanted to party. He said real soon he had a lot of serious work to do and he would be real busy. First we hit the town though , times three; Vegas, Manhattan and New Orleans in a ten day period.

When we got back Jesus sort of disappeared. I knew he was working on something big so mostly I just let him do his thing. I am close to Jesus and when he needs to work I leave him to it. end
