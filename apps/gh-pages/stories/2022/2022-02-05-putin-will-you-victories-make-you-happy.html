Putin; will your victories make you happy

FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

You honor Mr. Putin will your victories make you happy? Or like the great Warrior of old Asoka will you mourn for the suffering of the victors and vanquished in the Ukraine. Better to not start another War. Work instead on your enlightenment.

Chinese premier Xi Jinging avoid foreign entanglements. Stay out of squabbles half a world away.

President Biden avoid the war in Ukraine. Take your dogs for a walk instead.

World leaders as Aristotle said “the natural state of things is rest. Don’t start a war in the Ukraine.
