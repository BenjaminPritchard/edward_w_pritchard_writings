The Computer Who Went to Junior High-part 6

📅 2010-03-25 📖 139 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Craybo and I have agreed to drop out of junior high today. I was very proud of him. He got his grades and didn’t do all that well because of the problems at the beginning. However, I thought his essay on Rights of the Obese was very heart felt.

We have decided to finish the Grant project to instill human emotions and sensibilities into artificial intelligence at the Star Bucks in Kent. We both like the music there and Craybo likes the clientele especially. I put Craybo back in “A” logic mode and he has been playing a lot of video games with some of his school friends and has gotten interested in Japanese Haiku poetry which we do together sometimes. Gabby End part 6 End
