Read and enjoy Willie Tucker’s blues on the worksheet and then write a short poem [at least 5 lines] yourself. Notice and feel how Mr. Tucker reacted to the isolation on the moons of Jupiter and how it effected his art. Miss Donaldson

Ten year sentence Willie Tucker

I didn’t cheat so I was cheated I didn’t meet so I wasn’t met exposed to invisible forces I lost my reason, driven irrational I didn’t think, trying to feel I didn’t understand, chased by dreams I hid from my myself, then I died alone, crushed by volcano’s nature’s vindicator, volcano’s and nature ended abruptly my Two and a half years of a ten year sentence on Jupiter’s inconsequential moons. Remnants of me pulverized into dust properly mixed with Europa’s primordial debris shot into orbit around Jupiter conscious, but no longer moribund I circle the planet and long to decay my orbit and drift toward earth and fertilize the rain forests and lotus ponds and briefly re-bloom as a red and yellow water lily.
