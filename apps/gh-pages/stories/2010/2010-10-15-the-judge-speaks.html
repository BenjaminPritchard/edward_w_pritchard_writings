The Judge Speaks

📅 2010-10-15 📖 107 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
#rage
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

On the contrary Mr. Owens I do understand the situation. Your client shot and killed someone because they put on their blinker while driving going about their daily business. Because they had the misfortune to be in front of your client an innocent person is dead. And yes, I understand your client has been under a lot of stress. But I can’t overlook the fact that your client said that he had wanted to do the same thing before at least a thousand times to strangers he didn’t know. We will discuss sentencing later. This case confuses me. end
