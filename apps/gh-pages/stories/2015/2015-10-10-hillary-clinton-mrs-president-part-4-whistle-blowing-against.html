Hillary Clinton; Mrs. President, /part 4 whistle blowing against the Banking Industry

FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Author once chased around the hallowed halls of one of America’s too big to fail sacred New York Banks for a short distance and at a small time level observed the hypocrisy, deceitfulness and arrogance ” practiced” day to day and quarter to quarter under business as usual against the Public and for the malicious corporate insiders individual interests.

How will justice be served and the public interest be upheld when a cadre of well placed lawyers and judges diligently use the sacred common laws of commerce and precedent within the Banking Industry to spy upon and maintain secret malicious files against their customers, stockholders, public officials and critics?

Be assured Mrs. President that the New York banking insiders are currently secretly maintaining Gestapo style files against you, yourself, should you officially become the Country’s first female President and attempt to Judge the Banking industry in the common interest and uphold them to standards of common decency in some future monetary nightmare.

Support whistle blowing laws against Banks that are too big too fail by forcing Big banks and their cadres of stooge boosters such as Paul-son and associates et al of Wall Street to be held to the same performance standards of everyday prudent conduct and fiduciary responsibility that the Banking industry’s lawyers and Judge’s use to silence their whistle blowing employees and critics when defending themselves in court. Tit for tat and quid pro quo.

Big banks have no conscious, no heart and no soul. The only way to affect them is to make them pay and the only way to battle them in the fight for justice is to set the ground rules in advance before the Wall street banks have their backs to the wall over the latest financial crisis de jure.
