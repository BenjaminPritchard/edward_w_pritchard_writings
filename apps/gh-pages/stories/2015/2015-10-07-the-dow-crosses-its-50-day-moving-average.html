\"The\" Dow crosses it's 50 day moving average

📅 2015-10-07 📖 188 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Where is it going to? “The” Dow crosses it’s 50 day moving average. But where is it going next?

For a clue look at the Hang Seng. [ China]. Hey dude it’s more or less the same chart. What’s up with that? Type now DJI compare to HSI.

Why it’s a small world. But wait a minute.

Are the Chinese and the Americans of a similar temperament when it comes to looking at the world?

Odd, compare DJI to HSI; it’s the same chart. Who is running things anyway? and Where are they taking us?

Until now I liked the fact that at least the Chinese weren’t bombing the Syrians. [see previous post].

Sad; I see China will team up with the Russian coalition to bomb in Syria. I wonder if it’s [ the creation of a new China Russia bombing team] is reflected in the Dow, coincidentally which had a good day on the news?

And I had hoped that China had a Buddhist heart under that practical face it showed to the World.
