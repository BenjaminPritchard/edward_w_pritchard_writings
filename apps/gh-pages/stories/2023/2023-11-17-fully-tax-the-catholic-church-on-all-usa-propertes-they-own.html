fully tax the catholic church on all USA properties they own

FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

If I were President I would fully tax the catholic church and others on all the USA real estate properties they owned. Starting in twenty five years from the present. The money would be used appropriately to help the downtrodden. For a twenty five year period the tax would not start provided the catholic church and other churches went back and re reviewed their position on each religious schism or scriptual controversy they fought and or murdred people over, that is those who they intellectually diagreed with. In addition all religious organizations in the USA must publically forgive John Rawls for his positions on all matters they disagreed with him overs by admitting that in matters of opinion both sides are partially right and wrong at the same time.
