Will Anne Rice the Author go to Hell? part 1

📅 2013-10-16 📖 196 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Will Anne Rice the famous prolific modern American author go to Hell for her writing and lack of repentance? Two famous women Saints will be consulted for advise to her on how to avoid Hell. The two Saints, now dead, St Theresa and Saint Therese will be summoned as usual in these stories but will also be consulted through their writings. Anne Rice likewise will be judged in absentia.

Is hell a real place where the damned are eternally tormented with fires so hot to be indescribable? Must Anne Rice believe in Hell to go there? Can we judge Anne Rice to be a writer of some works of pornography? Who should Judge another? Is Hell real or a figment of imagination passed from generation to generation summoned by sickness, poverty, doubt and fear of death?

Is there a path to take to avoid Hell? Are sins rankable, with some worse than others? Should we sin in youth and repent in old age?

Why avoid Hell? What’s your risk in the questions concerning Hell’s significance.
