tomorrow’s news today again, America 11/05/2013

FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Americans have had enough of the political infighting caused by the two party system. Following the recent government “shutdown”, mainly the result of partisan finger pointing, the voters in America have devised a way to stop elected politicians from using party loyalty as a way to stymie creative thinking and strangle honest dealings.

Simply shocking. Proposition 129 is credited with implementing a new age of progress in American government. Proposition 129 passed 11/05/2013 made it illegal for a Congressman or Senator to refer to the name of either party, Republican or Democrat in open debate or lawmaking sessions. Finger pointing and blame shifting are radically reduced and some real progress in attacking the root problems hindering American society have occurred.

To put some teeth in the decree against a politician using the name of his party positively or the opponents party negatively a citizens arrest was allowed with a one thousand dollar award for each violation reported. Results are simply amazing.

Foul cried the politicians. It’s their fault said one Congressman pointing a finger. This is a terrorist assault on America values said a unanimous Senate.
