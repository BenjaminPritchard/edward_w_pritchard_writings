Officer Robinson is the complete package, me, I am just a trainee; in training here in Pakistan to be on the US team political prisoners division. I have got a lot to learn. I have finished my training in military history, ethics, and comparative religions. Thank goodness that is behind me. Now I can start my in field experience, where the action is so to speak. Officer Robinson is a tough boss but I am glad to learn from the best in the water boarding techniques. end
