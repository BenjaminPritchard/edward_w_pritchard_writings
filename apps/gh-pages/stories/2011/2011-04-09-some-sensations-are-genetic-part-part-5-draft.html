Holy Church buried the investigation into the matter of early morning awakenings disturbing and disrupting the activities of a Priest, a Bishop and an Arch Bishop. For seven years fact finding occurred, No expense was spared and no conclusions were ever reached.

Mya Johnstone the woman who started the process by having delusions of a small cat like predator striking her in the middle of the back refused to co-operate with the church’s investigation. Although a non practicing Catholic, Mya Johnstone did not want to expose her six year old daughter to the situation or the inquiry and publicity that would occur if it was realized that Mrs. Johnstone’s daughter was very much different than other children. In fact some experts felt that the daughter was unique genetically and the Mother had intuited the unborn child’s super natural abilities. Other experts felt it was just a typical genetic mutation.

Mother and daughter are well. Holy church refused comment.
