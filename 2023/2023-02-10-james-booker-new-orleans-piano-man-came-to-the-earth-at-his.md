# James Booker New Orleans  piano man came  to the earth at his second birth as a stand in for Jesus

James Booker New Orleans  piano man came  to the earth at his second birth as a second stand in for Jesus

fiction

edward w pritchard

Why would Jesus return to the earth for the second time as James Booker, the New Orleans piano prodigy? And why would Jesus return to the earth to full fill his prophesies of the second coming as a homo sexual drug addict? And why would the staff in the emergency room at the hospital in New Orleans refuse to treat James Booker when he was sitting in the hospital waiting to die of Kidney failure.

Was James Booker Jesus returned? Listen to James Booker's Oh Holy night rendition for clues. Do you need your Jesus to be perfect? Do you need your Jesus to be perfect, without sin,  and without human attributes and faults ?
