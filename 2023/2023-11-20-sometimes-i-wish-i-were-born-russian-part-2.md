sometimes I wish I were born Russian [ part 2]

A dozen years into the future from now there's these robots who are very intelligent and conniving and created by the Russian government to intervene with American society, politics, and institutions in a non violent but intrusive way.

Each robot looks and acts like a famous Russian writer or novelist and has one dictate from home in Moscow on how to behave  in America, " stay in character comrade".
