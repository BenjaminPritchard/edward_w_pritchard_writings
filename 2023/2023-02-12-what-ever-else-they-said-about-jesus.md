# what ever else they said about Jesus

whatever else they said about Jesus

fiction

edward w pritchard

Whatever else they said about Jesus, Jesus never sent important information to his twelve disciples by email.

Jesus never had twelve way phone conversations either. Face to face conversations is how Jesus communicated, whether to twelve people or an entire sermon on a mount.

We should take a lesson from Jesus' communications style. No phone chats and no emails.

Face to face conversations in business and personal life is how Jesus handled his personal life and business life.
