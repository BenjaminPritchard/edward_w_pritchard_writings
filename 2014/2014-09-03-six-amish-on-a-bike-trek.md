# Six Amish on a bike trek

fiction
Edward w Pritchard

Six Amish on a bike trip on the tow path.
White bonnets and straw hats trekking into the past.
Thin and sleek in old fashioned clothes like characters from a play.
Whenst goest thou noble pilgrims?
