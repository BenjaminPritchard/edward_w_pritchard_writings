story 5 of 11, I'll quit before I get that license

author continues with his goal of writing 11 short short stories in one sitting with no preparation or bullpen of ideas

Impossible. We are not in Mussolini’s Italy. I will not get a real estate license to write articles and stories for the New York magazines. Only some of my stories are about Real property and the entire idea is preposterous. I am a writer not a salesman. I have never sold a house nor have I wanted too. The bureaucrats have lost their sanity on this one. Still I do not know where to turn for assistance. Threats have been made against me and not just from the agents themselves. Yesterday was the last straw… to be continued
