it's just a matter of time

📅 2012-02-05 📖 75 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
#inquiry
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

It’s just a matter of time until you forget the time and date. A policeman or doctor stops you and asks what year is it. You are very tired or even sick today. What year? 2011 or 2012, or 1966. They have every right to question you here in front of them by chance, it had to happen. Where are they taking me now.
