don't buy a new car, house, or take that european vacation until you talk to pete best

📅 2022-07-27 📖 170 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Americans are scratching their heads as they watch all the large cap super duper stocks crash and with it their delusions about permanent prosperity and endless riches. Us baby boomers have gotten old and we are getting swept out along with our delusions and ideals.

Don’t despair ask original beatles drummer Pete Best who was replaced just before the fab four hit the big time what it’s like to be thrown out with the trash.

Watch your sacred stocks and investment like fine homes, expensive cars and meta stocks crash and throw you to the junk pile with them. Your old dude. It’s time for a new paradigm and your not invited.

I liked Ringo Star a lot but Pete Best didn’t seem like such a bad guy. Things have changed, try to make yourself comfortable baby boomer when you are uncerimoniously tossed on to the trash pile.
