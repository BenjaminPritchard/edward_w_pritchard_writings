Pretzel Pete of Hardy boys fame seeks a new author

FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Pretzel Pete of Hardy boys fame seeks a new author we hear. Pete who works the docks near Bay Port sometime in 1930’s America has always been a prime source of information for the Hardy boys Father detective Fenton Hardy.

America was a different place in the 1930’s said Pretzel Pete. ” I have seen people nearly kill for a pretzel during the great depression in America” said Pete. -and to be blunt it was because they were staving with no prospects of honest work, a steady job and the basic necessities of life.

How can Americans living in 2022 America possibly understand what it’s like to live in the great depression, or live through fascism in 1930’s Germany or survive the siege of Leningrad in Russia in WW2.

I must find a new author said Pete, ” this author Pritchard doesn’t have the words to awaken his meager audience.” Beware, the times are changing”. “Hot pretzels sang Pete, one now and two for your pockets for later”.
