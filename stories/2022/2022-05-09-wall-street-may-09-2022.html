Another debacle on Wall Street today. Could be the start of serious times.

Here’s today’s summary of today’s wall street action from Henry James in 1904, ” The American Scene” New York chapters.

” confusion carried to chaos for any intelligence”, any perception, any welter of sounds in which relief, detachment, dignity, meaning, perished utterly and lost all right. “

Couldn’t have said it better myself.
