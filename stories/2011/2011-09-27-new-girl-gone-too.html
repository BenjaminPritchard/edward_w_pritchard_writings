New Girl Gone too

📅 2011-09-27 📖 61 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
#color chart
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Down, down again to the sea of accumulated sorrow, to add a few drops of pain shared with you. The sea flashes blood red and then returns to a constant blue. Should you wish to return, excuse me if I don’t arise from the oceans of forgetfulness; rocking gently over purple waves.
