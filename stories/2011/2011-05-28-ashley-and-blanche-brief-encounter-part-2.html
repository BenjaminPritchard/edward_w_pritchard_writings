FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Mr. Pritchard we understand you are unrepresented by counsel. We represent the interests of Mr. Williams estate. Mrs Mitchell’s heirs have decided not to pursue the matter, preferring to let your article rest unread concerning characters created by Mrs. Mitchell. Frankly we are inclined to agree with them. Two characters of fiction from different historical eras. Their meeting is preposterous. In the future however do not reference characters created by Mr. Williams. end .
