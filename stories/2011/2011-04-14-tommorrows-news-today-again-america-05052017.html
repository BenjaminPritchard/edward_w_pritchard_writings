tomorrow’s news today again, America 05/05/2017

FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Simply shocking. Proposition 129 is credited with implementing an age of progress in American government. Proposition 129 passed 05/05/2017 made it illegal for a Congressman or Senator to refer to the name of either party, Republican or Democrat in open debate or lawmaking sessions. Finger pointing and blame shifting was radically reduced and some real progress in attacking the root problems hindering American society have occurred.

To put some teeth in the decree against a politician using the name of his party positively or the opponents party negatively a citizens arrest was allowed with a one thousand dollar award for each violation reported. Results are simply amazing.
