fly far

📅 2013-12-23 📖 49 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Fly far, fly low to the ground.

Soar over mountains, feel cold wind in cedars, hear splashing rain soaked butterfly, quenched by burning spattered with raindrops land in wildfires, rest on blazing golden boughs.

Fly far, land safely rest on golden boughs.
