to sea

📅 2013-10-29 📖 66 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Twice times tumbled, backward, rotating as I fell, from broken cliffs of shores place placid into teary waves and salty brine of seas future.

Falling and smacking, surfaced on frigid churned waters, sea birds soar, plunged in shouting waves squawking “courage”.

Stinging eyes open Search dim sky.

Exhausted arms, finished shoulders drag toward unseen refuge, distant hollow islands, forgotten.
