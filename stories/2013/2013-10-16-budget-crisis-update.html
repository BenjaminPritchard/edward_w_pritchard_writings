budget crisis, update

📅 2013-10-16 📖 57 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

What’s the greatest duty one person can owe to another? Teacher to student, employee to employer, Mother to Child? No, it’s a politician to those who elected them.

Do your duty elected United States politicians. Stop dithering, complete and pass the policy to temporarily solve the current debt crisis.
