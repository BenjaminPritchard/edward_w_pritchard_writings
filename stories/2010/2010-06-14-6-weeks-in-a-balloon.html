6 weeks in a balloon

📅 2010-06-14 📖 70 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Jersey spent six weeks peacefully floating along in her hot air balloon observing humanity as it went about it’s daily rituals and routines. It was comfortable and safe in the basket and once Jersey acclimated herself to the minor inconveniences she began to enjoy watching the people below as they went about the scurrying of their lives. end part 1
