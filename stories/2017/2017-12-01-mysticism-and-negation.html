mysticism and negation

📅 2017-12-01 📖 119 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

When I see the sky and the mountains, I never saw the face of God when I watched the two year old boy develop, first to the hunt When I suffered severely at loss, when I ached for the human condition, aging, death and banality When I dashed everyone’s hopes with tired practicality, when I was unsure, at loss for an answer, I smiled as I listened and being negative by nature and nurture suggested there might be another way, I see the sky and the mountains, I never saw the face of God except in the small Byzantine painting, I keep on the wall in the room where I sleep
