not fade away

📅 2026-07-13 📖 91 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot

Edward W Pritchard

Take a full draft of beer

Not fade away. Before we be dead.

Surely the song of me can’t end. How about you.

ignore the pains of age and suffering

Somehow we savor each day despite the pains,

its hard to recall if before was this or now is reality

Not fade away

end

reach for the glass /ed

Remember the Doctor warned you the limit of two beers

so we buy two cans of beers each holding two beers in each larger can
