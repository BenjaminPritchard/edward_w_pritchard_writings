Students are protesting again. Yawn.

Advice to students; don’t claim to be an anarchist- call yourself a libertarian.

Stay away from alcohol [ see Eugene O’ Neil’s “Iceman Cometh” Hickey as the personification of Alcoholism.

Men, get some work experience, major in something that interests you and get some work experience

Professors, you work for the college, you are part of the establishment.

Football players on scholarships and other athletes, get some advice on life from an older persons you trust

alumni- move on

the cost of College- invest wisely

on line college students- click here for solidarity-a [$25. 00 fee will apply]

student loans- confusing really, see below

bottom line- look into the future and then decide what to major in, consider such factors as job demand in Asia, your personal health, probable retirement date and lifespan, marriage and family situation and your nature, personality and philosophically if persons really have a ” self” and if it matters in a capitalistic society, also consider stock prices 20 years hence, the cost of capital and inflation.
