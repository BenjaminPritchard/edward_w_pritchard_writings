To Hillary Clinton; Mrs President, / part 2, re sustainability

📅 2015-10-08 📖 180 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

With fears abounding about global warming and individuals causing an overly large footprint on the environment perhaps we can use pro-active forms of a negative income tax or lump sum payments to help the environment, alleviate some of the carbon footprint left by citizens, and make people happy in a sustainable lifestyle that is healthy and wholesome.

We are talking here about encouraging citizens to adopt a nomadic lifestyle to follow reindeer or herds of buffalo and to use the animals to provide the majority of the groups food, shelter and clothing.

Government fiscal policy could be used to initially to recruit persons to join the new lifestyle.

Perhaps citizens of Alaska or Native Americans on reservations would best initially respond to the new program.

Once successful and if a large enough number of persons were following the nomadic lifestyle a sustainable enjoyable wholesome life style might be available to all Americans who wished to travel and make smaller their carbon footprint upon the Earth.
