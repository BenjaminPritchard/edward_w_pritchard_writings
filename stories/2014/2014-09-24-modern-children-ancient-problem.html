Modern children, ancient problem

📅 2014-09-24 📖 118 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

Issue:

The Greek city states in the classical period emphasized that individual interests were subordinate to the State and the common good. The individual was expected to stay busy with collective activity.

In India in ancient times many people withdrew into their minds away from the world and society with religious contemplation.

America now. Who watches the children while both parents work long hours pursuing materialistic achievement, personal actualization, and the approval of contemporary society.

Modern children, ancient problem. How has feminism and a global economy altered traditional roles for contemporary American Parents?

Day to day many challenges are forgotten to History, ignored as too mundane to be remembered.
