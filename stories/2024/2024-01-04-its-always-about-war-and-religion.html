it’s always about war and religion

FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

It’s always about war and religion. And belligerent young men killing women and helpless children.

Currently Israel is desimating Gaza for a sneak attack against ordinary souls going about their own lives.

Israel in the Middle East is always the super power that is in the way of Muslim locals. Young local male Muslim locals race across the borders through tunnels armed with various weapons and cause temporary loss of life to Israel

and its inhabitants. Then using superior weapons Israel methodically destroys it’s enemy.

Is it about war or is it about religious animosity. Muslims and Jews, I am a back sliding protestant who always admired the Muslims. Of late though I have admired John Lennon’s proclamation that religion causes wars.
