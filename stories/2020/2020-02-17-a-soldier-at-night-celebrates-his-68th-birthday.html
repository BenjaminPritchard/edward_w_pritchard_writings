a soldier at night celebrates his 68th birthday

📅 2020-02-17 📖 77 words ⏱️ ~1 min read ✍️ edward pritchard
🔗 Original Blogspot
FICTION
EDWARD W PRITCHARD

the night is clear but the Moon is always at the quarter a soldier lost in dark night celebrates his 68th birthday his fingers aching from scratching at the hard frozen earth to bury himself in for cover from angry low flying rifle bullets randomly fired by unseen enemies oh for a tattered flag or soft woman to wrap himself in until next light
