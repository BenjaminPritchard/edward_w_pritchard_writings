# melancholy dancer

fiction
edward w pritchard

smile, it's removed melancholy dancer

swing your partner, look away their gone, tread on fair flatfoot

propinquity as one hand clapping

all you love, you loved alone

a dream in a dream

a whisper into the well of innocent melancholy

maintain your stone henge of sorrow

be sad, it makes you happy

your pyramid endures
