# first date, two gay women

fiction
edward w pritchard

Patient, slow, and genuine.
So many questions
and answers,
long answers.
Listen to every nuance.
Proceed cautiously but methodically.
Lean forward, probe for details.
Talk about feelings.
Then hint at past hurts.
Eavesdropper,
learn how to talk to a woman.
