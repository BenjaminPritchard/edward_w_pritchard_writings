# Carmen Electra and Jascha Heifetz

fiction
edward w pritchard

Jascha Heifetz playing Tchaikovsky's Violin Concerto in D Major with a world class full orchestra backing him up at Carnegie hall. Still he commands the stage with a once in a life time performance. The epitome of human potential in music.

Carmen Electra with the Pussy Cat dolls doing Santa Baby in costume. Despite the breath taking talent of the other performers Carmen Electra commands the stage. All can can dancers are beautiful but Carmen Electra is one in a million.

Surely it's true that man is the measure of all things.
