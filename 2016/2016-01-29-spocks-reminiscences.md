# Spock's reminiscences

fiction
edward w pritchard

Me and Gemini orbits corrupting
no more intersections, no more gravitational pull
drifting apart in the vastness of space
Gemini a cold dwarf star
me a lifeless planet
vast universe,
timeless
entropy
