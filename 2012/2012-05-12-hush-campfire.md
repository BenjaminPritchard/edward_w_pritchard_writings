# Hush campfire

fiction
edward w pritchard

Hush campfire,
wood don't crackle and pop,
silence moon,
still night wind.
I hear her on the steep mountain pass.
She puts her palms to her knees to help with the arduous climb.
Still heart,
what does she call?
