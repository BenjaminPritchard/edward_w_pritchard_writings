# straining to raise a family

fiction
edward w pritchard

For those of you straining and struggling to raise a family, hearken not to disheartening thoughts and sentiments. Time is quickly flying by and soon you will be alone. Enjoy the moment, relish the joy and sorrow of everyday things.

Crammed four to a seat in the rear of a rusting station wagon it's difficult to see any purpose to existence.

Looking back from afar you surmise that "your sorrows are your joys" [1]. Enjoy the moment. Tempus fugat.

[1] Steve Winwood, and Jim Cripaldi,  Traffic "Low spark of high heeled boys"
