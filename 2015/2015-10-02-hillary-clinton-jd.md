# Hillary Clinton JD

fiction
edward w pritchard

I wanted to warn future Congress and The Senate that when Hillary Clinton gets elected not to put her on cross, that is cross examination over her past dealings. Not because she is the new President, or a strong woman but because she has a Doctor of the Laws degree from Yale.

From experience some of us know that a woman lawyer likes to ask the questions and when it comes her turn to be under cross examination they have a short memory and often although they have no independent recollection of the events under discussion if they do recall anything they recall that nothing like that ever really happen, ipso facto.

Still women lawyers, don't we just Love them anyway.
