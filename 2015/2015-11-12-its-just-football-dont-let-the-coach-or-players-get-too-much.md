# it's just football; don't let the coach or players get too much in the news

fiction
edward w pritchard

It's just football; don't let the coach or players too get too much in the news. The world is too much with us late and soon sayeth the poet. Author has heard enough about problems involving football for now and has decided to go back to worrying about an enormous streaking pulsating meteor from space striking the planet and ending higher forms of life and reorienting the local landscape.

Here's what I wrote before about football coaches:

##  SUNDAY, JANUARY 1, 2012

###  a football coach must ignore the scenery

a football coach must ignore the scenery

fiction
edward w pritchard

Coach Williams was once a surveillance pilot in Viet Nam. One thing Coach learned as a pilot that served him well as a Football Coach was a football coach must ignore the scenery. Coach Williams was never caught on National camera gawking at the petite Japanese Cheerleader doing acrobatic maneuvers at the college where he coached. Not once was coach embarrassed at a  New  Years Bowl game for staring at the Cheerleaders. While working Coach stuck to business; what Coach did in Private is not known.
