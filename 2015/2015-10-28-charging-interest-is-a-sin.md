# charging interest is a sin

fiction
edward w pritchard

Sometimes old ways are better; charging interest is a sin. Too many complications result to the detriment of society by the conniving[s] of ambitious sorts looking to rise at the expense of their neighbors.

Step back and contemplate on the matter anew. How many social evils spin forth from the charging of interest and the worship of an expanding money supply?
