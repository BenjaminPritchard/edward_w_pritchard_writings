# Laura Nyro; the feminine mystique and creative success

fiction
edward w pritchard

Look for a woman as a prominent person in World  literature and more than likely she comes out as Laura Petrarch's muse or Dante's Beatrice. Why haven't creative women played a more memorable role on the stage of the famous personages of History?

Look no further than the career of American songwriter and singer Laura Nyro for answer to above query. Nyro creative genius and innovator flew under the radar of American success and mass popularity in the music business.

When Laura Nyro did eventually win a place in the rock and Roll hall of fame more as a song writer than popular singer it was not without controversy and rolling of eyes that she didn't deserve the honor.

Listen to Laura Nyro's rendition of the song she wrote " Stoney End". Exquisite

Today so many male and female singers of marginal talent record myriad forgettable songs. Yet they dominate the mass culture and rake in the royalties.

Give genius it's due. There's something fascinating about a creative and talented Woman. Listen to the songs written and recorded by Laura Nyro, singer, song writer, piano player. Exquisite.
