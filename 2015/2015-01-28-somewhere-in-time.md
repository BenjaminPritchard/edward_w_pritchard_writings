# somewhere in time

fiction
Edward w Pritchard

Embers die hard
but our campfire is gone.
Given up the wait,
I am somewhere in time
gone, forgotten,
left for dead.
Poking the fire with a forked stick
listen to the scraping,
rattle future, present, forgotten past
across the universe
hear glowing embers simmer.
