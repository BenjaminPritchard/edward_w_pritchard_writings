# To Hillary Clinton; Mrs President/part 3, attack the real issue

fiction
edward w pritchard

Madame President, so often well intentioned Government programs do not attack the real problem at hand. The reasons are varied.

As an example prison reform. What is the best way to improve the happiness of unfortunate prisoners while they are incarcerated. It's to send willing women in for conjugal visits.

Here in my Home town of Akron, Ohio what do we most need to improve the general level of happiness? No it's not to outlaw begging at the side of expressway entrances.

What we need most here in Akron, Ohio is a really cool bridge across the Ohio Canal like the bridge of sighs in Venice Italy or a bridge like the Ponte Vecchio in Florence. If we had such a bridge the general happiness would be raised and the citizens would be more self confident and industrious leading our area to a new re-burst of creativity and artistic accomplishment.
