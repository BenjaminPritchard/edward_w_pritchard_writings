# Green Stamps and China
China tries to join the World Economic Community but the USA objects

fiction
edward w pritchard

Part 1
A Very ordinary tradgedy

A pretty girl with a disability, putting on lipstick with shaking hands, lonely and waiting to talk to the young man who is working as a gatekeeper at a public gymnasium. He checking tickets, allowing only members in, her standing sadly alone, as he finds imaginary jobs to do between customers to avoid the handicapped girl, who waits and waits and refuses to be insulted.
