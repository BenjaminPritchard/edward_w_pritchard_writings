# No one wears a suit to work anymore

fiction
edward w pritchard

No one wears a suit to work anymore and the country's gone to hell because of it.

Must be because of all the women who entered the work force. Still they did look good in their suits. Now that they got the jobs wages keep plunging.

Maybe the Country's gone to hell because the students don't dress properly for school. They don't respect their elders and don't understand they have to suffer and prepare if they are going to make it.

Old men don't lead anymore. Heck they don't even play golf much, at least not in appropriate numbers to keep the courses in business. They just go to the gym and sit on the machines to rest.

No one wears a suit to church anymore, in fact no goes to church anymore it's too crowded,[attributed in modern times to Mr Berra] maybe that's why the country's gone to hell.

I wonder do people still get a new suit to get buried in. That would help, then people could work at the mall again.

No one wears a suit to work anymore because all business is done on the internet. You buy your insurance on the internet and don't need an agent so he won't be at the mall or church , and we buy our books on the internet , skip that the internet tells us what to read

no one wears a suit to work anymore, where does everyone work anyway? Maybe other states are doing better

media isn't honest with us, cable's too expensive and there's nothing to watch, and magazines are full of young people inventing sex for the first time, it's quite a scandal don't tell our great grand father

No one wears a suit to work anymore, maybe I'll wear a suit to work today, skip that I am too fat to fit it, maybe the gym.
