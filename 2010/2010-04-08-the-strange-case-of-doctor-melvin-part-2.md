Setting A major University actually Kent State School of Business-Year is 2018

Gabby's Office is dream like. It houses a large masculine super Computer trying to evolve into being human by developing sensibilities and emotions. Occasionally she rides an exercise bike stage left. The trading room is bowl like and is visible just off stage. Upstage on wall is a picture of a beautiful Ivy League type girl a former lover of Gabby who she has walked out on twice. Hank's bar is a three story building with a bar on second floor up a flight of steps and the third floor is a hippy style pad where people mysteriously crash when sleepy or needing to have significant dreams.

Cast
Gabby-now 38,troubled but brilliant,gay,ultra successful,un-corporate,a beat or goth
Melvin-Gabby's 18 year old assistant, typical young man from Midwest-brilliant in science and math, lacking in experience and humanities, falls in love, typical love triangle develops, twist is jealous original love is a super computer[female]
Craybo- Gabby co-programmed this super computer, he is male, see other stories above for him, went to junior high to develop human emotions- he is famous world wide, "Father" of Milly
Milly-Craybo's offspring, super computer- female- can be either Medea type or Electra depending on story sponsor's choice and preferences of plot device
Kasie-typical blond large chested blue eyed beauty, Milly hates her initially,, depending on plot preferred she is either Medea or Electra by end of story

Time Feb 5, 2018-five months have passed since Melvin became Gabby's assistant and we are in the middle of the second Semester of Melvin's freshman year at Kent State. Time moves in all directions delusionally in this author's stories.
