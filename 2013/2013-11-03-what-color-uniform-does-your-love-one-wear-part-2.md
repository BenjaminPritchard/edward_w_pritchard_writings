# what color uniform does your love one wear/ part 2

what color uniform does you love one wear/ part 2

Feminism I support. A woman President, woman in the military providing they don't get hurt and equal education and rights and opportunities for women of all ages.

For you though dear there is one thing, just for you mind you. The cigarette girl. I mentioned it once. The cigarette girl with the half sailor's white uniform with a pink sash looped across your bare shoulders attached to the flat red velvet covered tray pushed against your curving waist supporting a bottle of  napoleon brandy with two heated crystal brandy sifters and scattered cigarettes, cigars and the exotic thin sliced fruits on the tray. Walk slow when you enter the smoky room, take your time, I have been waiting a long time. Just for you, mind you.
