# Bessie Smith and Fiona Apple - After You've Gone

fiction
edward w Pritchard

Author has criticized modern girl singers for spending too much time recording their own timely material and ignoring classic works, classic in terms of blues, jazz, pops, etc.

As an ata boy Author wishes to compliment Fiona Apple's rendition of "After Your Gone" a studio rehearsal version on you tube.

Start

Bessie Smith and Fiona Apple- "After your Gone"

Musical preference in the hearing of an individual song by the listener is a matter of personal taste and as the sage says, " there is no accounting for taste". Having said that we would like to praise the excellent rendition of "After your Gone" by Fiona Apple.

Using Bessie Smith's performance of " After your Gone" as the Canonical version listen to the emotion and verity displayed by Fiona Apple in singing " After your Gone". Stupendous .

Fiona Apple is a great talent. Please record more classics from the great woman singers of the twentieth century. I enjoyed your performance of " After your Gone" very much. Each time I see the performance currently available on you tube I enjoy hearing it more and more and am awed by your talent.
end
