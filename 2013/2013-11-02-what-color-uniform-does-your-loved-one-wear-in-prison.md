# What color uniform does your loved one wear in prison

fiction
edward w Pritchard

What color uniform does your loved one wear in prison. Should the national government regulate the colors of the clothes American citizens wear in prison?  Federalism or states rights when it comes to how to dress the 2.2 Americans that are serving in prison?

Some prisoners in the toughest prisons in America can wear blue jeans and tee shirts, Other inmates at more humane institutions wear gray or orange uniforms. No national regulations exist in this matter of what color uniform does your loved one wear in prison. Other issues related to prison rights sometimes are overlooked in some prisons, some States, and for some prisoners.

States rights or Federalism? How should America regulate and govern its citizens who are in prison?
