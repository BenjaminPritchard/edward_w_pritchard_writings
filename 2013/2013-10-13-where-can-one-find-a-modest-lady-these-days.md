# where can one find a modest lady these days

fiction
edward w pritchard

Where can one find a modest lady these days? A woman who softly lowers her eyes when she is stared at. Modesty in a woman is a virtue.

Where can one find a gentleman these days? A man who doesn't leer at women inappropriately or untimely. A gentleman is rare these days.
