# Michelle Obama, in the White House

fiction
edward w pritchard

Author wishes to compliment the fine job as First lady done by Michelle Obama and previously by Laura Bush. Both women are excellent role models, intelligent and have a commanding presence about themselves; like a Queen, but an acceptable muted American type Queen.

Hopefully Mrs. Obama is advising her husband this week. As a Harvard educated lawyer she must have something to add to the debate over what to do in American Government this week and one would expect throughout the remainder of his term.

It's time for this Country to have a woman President; Mrs. Obama or someone like her.
The time has come to look at things from a unique perspective never tried before in American politics.
