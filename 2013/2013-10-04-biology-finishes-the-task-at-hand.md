# Biology finishes the task at hand
repost/edit

fiction
edward w pritchard

We claim credit for our endeavor when it's really gravity that does most productive work. Even when we swing an axe or sledge hammer after our muscles stop pushing gravity completes the task.

We claim credit for our initiative when we follow through with a plan and complete a procedure that earns extra interest on our funds or grows our portfolio when it's really time that adds the final smidgen; and gives us a nice cumulative investment return.

We claim credit when we shave and put on men's cologne, or a women claims the credit when they put on pink lipstick and wear a tight skirt and complete a seduction but it's really biology that  completes the task at hand and joins couples together.

Biology finishes the task at hand of seduction.
